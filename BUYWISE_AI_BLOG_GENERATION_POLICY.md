# BuyWise AI Blog Content & Automated Generation Standards

## Policy Overview
This document establishes the official editorial guidelines, SEO structure, Google Helpful Content (Anti-Spam) standards, E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) criteria, and affiliate link management rules for all BuyWise AI blog articles and AI-generated shopping guides.

---

## 1. Compliance with Google Helpful Content & Anti-Spam Guidelines

1. **Value-First Editorial Tone**: Articles must prioritize reader utility, objective comparison, product specifications, real-world comfort/performance factors, and buyer checklists. Articles must **never** read like aggressive sales pitches or low-quality thin content.
2. **Clear Affiliate Disclosures**: Every shopping guide featuring store links must display a prominent disclosure:
   > *Affiliate Disclosure: As an Amazon Associate, BuyWise AI earns from qualifying purchases made via links tagged with our Store ID (`pajonline-21`).*
3. **Live Price Variability Warnings**: Always state clearly that prices, discounts, ratings, seller policies, and stock availability change rapidly and must be verified against live retailer product listings before purchase.
4. **No Misleading Claims**: Avoid guaranteed discount claims or unverified claims regarding third-party products.

---

## 2. Google E-E-A-T Requirements

* **Experience**: Provide practical advice on real-world usage (e.g., fabric weight for dancing, battery endurance under compiler workloads, weight/portability, care instructions).
* **Expertise**: Include detailed specification breakdowns (CPU cores, fabric composition, mirror work placement, lining quality).
* **Authoritativeness**: Frame advice using structured comparison tables, realistic budget bands, and 8-step buyer checklists.
* **Trustworthiness**: Highlight pros, cons, potential drawbacks, and return/exchange considerations for every product or style featured.

---

## 3. SEO Architecture Standards

Every article generated or published must follow this 11-section architecture:

1. **H1 Title**: Search-intent focused title with year / query targets (e.g., *Garba Dress Comparison Guide 2026*).
2. **Subtitle & Value Tagline**: Highlight BuyWise AI motto (*Find Your Perfect Look with BuyWise AI — Shop Smarter. Buy Better.*).
3. **Introduction**: Address common buyer dilemmas (budget vs premium, style choices, comfort considerations).
4. **Markdown Comparison Table**: Structured matrix mapping product styles/models against visual appeal, comfort/performance, and ideal user fit.
5. **Featured Amazon Candidate Cards**: Direct store product candidates with titles, key strengths, price bands, and Amazon affiliate links containing Store ID `pajonline-21`.
6. **Detailed Deep-Dive Sections (H2/H3)**: Breakdowns of major sub-categories with advantages, drawbacks, and pre-purchase check items.
7. **Budget Comparison Bands (INR ₹)**: Realistic price bands (Under ₹1,000, ₹1,000–₹2,000, ₹2,000–₹3,500, Above ₹3,500) and shopping priorities.
8. **8-Step Practical Buying Guide**: Step-by-step instructions for online shopping (sizing, return policies, final cost comparison).
9. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**: Schema-friendly Answers to top user search queries.
10. **Final Verdict**: Objective closing summary encouraging informed comparison on BuyWise AI.
11. **SEO Publishing Details**: Title, Meta Description, URL Slug, Primary/Secondary Keywords, Category, Alt Text, Excerpt.
