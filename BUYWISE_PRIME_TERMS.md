## 1. Membership Overview
BuyWise Prime is a digital membership program providing enhanced shopping intelligence, priority AI Virtual Try-On rendering, instant price drop notifications, and exclusive partner brand discounts.

## 2. Plans & Billing
- **FREE**: Standard access to SmartCompare, public coupons, and standard VTO.
- **PRIME MONTHLY**: ₹199 / month (Recurring monthly billing).
- **PRIME YEARLY**: ₹1,499 / year (Recurring annual billing).

## 3. Cancellation & Renewal
- Memberships auto-renew at the end of each billing period unless auto-renew is cancelled prior to the renewal date.
- Cancelling auto-renew preserves Prime benefits until the end of the current paid billing period (`expiresAt`).

## 4. Refund Policy
- Verified refunds processed through server-to-server webhook events immediately revoke Prime benefits.
- Refund requests are subject to BuyWise Customer Support review and provider verification.

## 5. Cross-Platform Entitlement
- A single BuyWise Account entitlement is valid across both Web and Android applications.
- Users must sign into the same authenticated BuyWise account to sync entitlement.
