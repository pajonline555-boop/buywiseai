# BUYWISE MAYA LANGUAGE POLICY

**PROJECT**: BuyWise AI  
**PHASE**: 11.2  
**DOCUMENT**: Multilingual Answering Policy (English, Hindi, Hinglish)  

---

## 1. LANGUAGE SELECTION ARCHITECTURE

Maya respects the user's active application language as configured in `LanguageManager` / `i18nContext`:

1. **English (`en`)**: All generated explanations in standard English.
2. **Hindi (`hi`)**: All explanations in Devnagari Hindi script.
3. **Hinglish (`hinglish`)**: All explanations in natural Roman-script Hinglish.

---

## 2. PROPER NAME PRESERVATION RULE

The following proper nouns, brand names, and technical terms remain unchanged across ALL languages:

- `BuyWise AI`
- `Maya`
- `SmartCompare`
- `Amazon`
- `Flipkart`
- `Myntra`
- `Meesho`
- `eBay`
- `Ajio`
- `Nykaa`
- `Try-On`

---

## 3. NO MIXING RULE

- **English Mode**: Zero Hindi/Hinglish words mixed in.
- **Hindi Mode**: Native Devnagari script for all explanatory text.
- **Hinglish Mode**: Fluid Roman-script conversational Hinglish.

---

## 4. CODE EXAMPLES

### English Mode:
> *"You can participate in the Weekly BuyWise Fashion Challenge by opening the featured challenge in BuyWise, using the eligible featured product with Try-On, and submitting your result."*

### Hindi Mode:
> *"आप BuyWise के Weekly BuyWise Fashion Challenge में भाग लेने के लिए ऐप में दिए गए सक्रिय प्रतियोगिता को खोलें, उपलब्ध featured product का Try-On करें और अपना परिणाम जमा करें।"*

### Hinglish Mode:
> *"BuyWise ke Weekly Fashion Challenge mein participate karne ke liye active challenge open karein, featured product ka Try-On karein aur apna result submit karein."*
