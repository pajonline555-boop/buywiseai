# BuyWise AI — Premium Brand Guidelines

**Version**: Phase 9.9  

---

## 1. Visual Aesthetics & Styling Rules

- **Spacious Layouts**: Generous padding, dark mode glassmorphism (`#090715`), subtle gold borders (`rgba(255, 215, 0, 0.3)`).
- **Restrained Badging**: Allowed badges include `BUYWISE SELECT`, `RED CARPET EDIT`, `EXECUTIVE EDIT`, `SIGNATURE`, `OCCASION EDIT`, `VERIFIED SELLER`, `PREMIUM PICK`.
- **Prohibited Badges & Copy**: Never display "BEST IN INDIA", "#1 LUXURY PRODUCT", "CELEBRITY APPROVED", or "GUARANTEED LUXURY" without independent proof.

---

## 2. Copywriting Guidelines

- **BuyWise Select**: "Selected for the Way You Shop" — "Thoughtfully curated styles, essentials and standout finds."
- **Red Carpet Edit**: "Step Into the Spotlight" — "Statement styles for evenings, celebrations and unforgettable occasions."
- **Executive Edit**: "Made for the Moment That Matters" — "Refined essentials for work, travel and everyday confidence."

---

## 3. Score Separation & Transparency

- **Merchandising Badge**: Indicates thematic curation.
- **Smart Value Score**: Indicates value/price ratio.
- **Shopping Trust Score**: Indicates seller reliability.
- **Product Source**: Always shows Amazon, Flipkart, BuyWise Store, or Partner Fulfilled.
