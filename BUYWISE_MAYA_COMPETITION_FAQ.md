# BUYWISE MAYA COMPETITION FAQ SPECIFICATION

**PROJECT**: BuyWise AI  
**PHASE**: 11.2  
**DOCUMENT**: Weekly BuyWise Fashion Challenge FAQ Specification  

---

## 1. COMPETITION LIFECYCLE STAGES

The Weekly BuyWise Fashion Challenge follows a controlled 9-stage lifecycle:

1. `DRAFT` — Admin drafting phase.
2. `SCHEDULED` — Challenge announced with upcoming start date.
3. `LIVE` — Active submission window open for users.
4. `SUBMISSION_CLOSED` — Submissions locked; pending moderation review.
5. `VOTING_LIVE` — Community voting period active for approved submissions.
6. `VOTING_CLOSED` — Voting locked; vote verification in progress.
7. `UNDER_VERIFICATION` — Anti-fraud and vote validation checks.
8. `WINNER_DECLARED` — Official winner announced on Home & Leaderboard.
9. `ARCHIVED` — Challenge historical archive.

---

## 2. SUBMISSION LIFECYCLE

`SUBMITTED` → `UNDER_REVIEW` → `APPROVED` → `VOTING` → `WINNER` / `FEATURED`

- Unapproved or rejected submissions do not enter public voting.

---

## 3. AUTHORITATIVE COMPETITION Q&A MATRIX

### Q: How can I take part in the BuyWise competition?
**Answer**: You can participate in the Weekly BuyWise Fashion Challenge by opening the featured challenge in BuyWise, using the eligible featured product with Try-On, creating your Try-On result, and submitting it during the active submission period if you want to enter the competition. Your submission may be reviewed before it becomes eligible for voting.

### Q: Is entry free?
**Answer**: If the currently active competition is configured as Free Entry, there is no purchase requirement to enter. Maya checks the current competition configuration before confirming this.

### Q: Do I need to buy the product to participate?
**Answer**: BuyWise competitions should not require a purchase unless the official rules for that specific competition explicitly say otherwise. Please check the current competition terms.

### Q: How is the winner selected?
**Answer**: After voting closes, valid votes are verified. The winner is determined according to the official rules of that competition.

### Q: Can paid votes be purchased?
**Answer**: No. BuyWise's competition design does not use paid votes or require purchases for voting unless an officially published competition rule specifically states otherwise.

### Q: What happens to my photo?
**Answer**: BuyWise treats Try-On media as private by default. A competition submission requires an explicit user action/permission. Only an approved submission may become publicly visible according to the competition rules.
